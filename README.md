# Regan-Temudo-GRC-Portfolio

Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Portfolio Repository  
Author: Regan Temudo  

This repository showcases practical, hands-on GRC projects aligned with real-world compliance and risk management expectations.  

The objective is to demonstrate capability in:

- Policy development  
- Risk assessment  
- Framework mapping  
- Compliance documentation  
- Business-focused security communication  

This portfolio is designed to support applications for:

- GRC Intern  
- Junior GRC Analyst  
- Compliance Documentation Analyst  
- Information Security Analyst (GRC Focus)  

---

# Portfolio Structure

## 1. Security Policies

Located in `/policies/`

Includes:

- Information Security Policy  
- Acceptable Use Policy  
- Access Control Policy  
- Incident Response Policy  
- Data Classification Policy  
- Password & Authentication Policy  

These policies are written for a fictional fintech organization (50 employees) and aligned with ISO 27001 and NIST CSF principles.

---

## 2. Risk Assessment

Located in `/risk-assessment/`

Includes:

- Comprehensive Risk Register  
- 15–20 identified risks  
- Likelihood and Impact scoring  
- Risk rating calculation  
- Treatment plan (Mitigate / Accept / Transfer / Avoid)  

Demonstrates structured risk management methodology.

---

## 3. Framework Mapping (Gap Analysis)

Located in `/framework-mapping/`

Includes:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework Mapping  
- Identification of existing controls  
- Gap identification  
- Recommended remediation actions  

Optional advanced section:
- ISO 27001 Annex A Control Mapping  

Demonstrates audit-readiness assessment capability.

---

## 4. Executive Risk Briefs

Located in `/executive-briefs/`

Includes:

- One-page executive summaries  
- Technical risk translated into business impact  
- Clear recommended actions  

Demonstrates business communication skills essential for GRC roles.
