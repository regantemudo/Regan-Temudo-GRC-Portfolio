# Acceptable Use Policy

**Organisation:** FinTech Co  
**Document ID:** POL-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** March 2026  
**Review Date:** March 2027  
**Owner:** CISO  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Framework Alignment:** ISO/IEC 27001 (A.8) | NIST CSF (PR.AT)

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the acceptable use of FinTech Co's information systems, devices, networks, and data. It exists to protect employees, the company, and its customers from harm arising from misuse of technology assets.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and third parties who access FinTech Co systems or data whether on company-owned or personal (BYOD) devices.

---

## 3. Acceptable Use

### 3.1 General Principles

- Company systems and data must be used primarily for legitimate business purposes.
- Employees must exercise the same professional judgement when using company systems as they would in any other business activity.
- Users are responsible for all activity conducted under their credentials.

### 3.2 Internet & Email Use

- Internet access is provided for business use. Reasonable personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, consume excessive bandwidth, or compromise security.
- Employees must not access, download, or distribute content that is illegal, offensive, or unrelated to business in a manner that could expose the company to reputational or legal risk.
- Work email accounts must not be used to subscribe to personal mailing lists or conduct personal business.
- Employees must not click links or open attachments from unknown or suspicious senders. Suspected phishing emails must be reported to IT Security immediately.

### 3.3 Software & Applications

- Only software approved by the IT team may be installed on company devices. Installation of unapproved software, browser extensions, or plugins is prohibited.
- Employees must not bypass or disable security controls including antivirus, firewalls, or endpoint protection.
- The use of personal cloud storage (e.g. personal Dropbox, Google Drive) to store company or customer data is prohibited. Approved cloud storage platforms must be used.

### 3.4 Data Handling

- Company and customer data must only be stored on approved, encrypted systems.
- Sensitive data must not be shared via personal email, messaging apps, or public channels.
- Printed documents containing confidential information must be stored securely and shredded when no longer needed.

### 3.5 Remote Work & BYOD

- Employees working remotely must connect via the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Personal devices used for work (BYOD) must have screen locks, device encryption, and up-to-date operating systems enabled.
- Company data must not be stored on personal devices beyond what is necessary for the immediate task.

### 3.6 Social Media

- Employees must not share confidential company information, customer data, or unreleased product details on social media.
- Employees may discuss their role and employer in a professional manner but must make clear they are not speaking on behalf of FinTech Co unless formally authorised.

---

## 4. Unacceptable Use

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

- Accessing, transmitting, or storing illegal content of any kind
- Circumventing or disabling security controls
- Attempting to gain unauthorised access to systems, accounts, or data
- Installing or distributing malware or unauthorised software
- Using company systems for personal financial gain or operating a personal business
- Sharing credentials with any other person
- Using company resources to harass, bully, or discriminate against any individual
- Connecting unapproved external devices (USB drives, personal hotspots) to company systems without IT approval

---

## 5. Monitoring & Privacy

FinTech Co reserves the right to monitor use of company systems, devices, and networks to ensure compliance with this policy and applicable law. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-owned equipment or networks.

Monitoring will be conducted in accordance with applicable data protection legislation and will be proportionate to the risk.

---

## 6. Reporting Violations

Suspected violations of this policy must be reported to the IT Security team or via the internal whistleblowing channel. Reports made in good faith will be treated confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who reports a violation in good faith will not be tolerated.

---

## 7. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Revocation of system access
- Disciplinary action, up to and including termination
- Referral to law enforcement where illegal activity is involved
